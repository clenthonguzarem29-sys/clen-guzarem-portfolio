SEO Tools & Approach

Tools and workflows I use or understand across on-page, technical, local SEO, keyword research, and reporting.

On-Page SEO

For on-page SEO, the best place to work is WordPress with a free SEO plugin, either Rank Math or Yoast SEO. These let us edit titles, meta descriptions and focus keywords, and they also handle XML sitemaps, schema markup, redirects, canonical settings and breadcrumbs.

WordPressRank MathYoast SEO

Technical SEO

Screaming Frog crawls a site and finds broken links, redirect chains, missing or duplicate titles and metas, and heading problems. Ahrefs supports site audit plus internal link and backlink data checks. Google Search Console helps identify indexing issues, sitemaps, Core Web Vitals, and query-level clicks and positions.

Performance Measurement

How I Report

My approach to gathering data, evaluating performance, and translating reporting into practical recommendations.

I would gather data from Google Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile, and the keyword-tracking tool.

I would summarize organic traffic, impressions, clicks, average position, keyword movement, conversions or leads, and local-search performance where relevant. I would also include completed SEO actions during the month and any issues identified.

Then I would explain the results in simple language: what improved, what declined, why it may have happened, and what actions I recommend for the next month. My goal is to make the report clear, accurate, and useful for the client.
